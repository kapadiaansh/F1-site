METHODOLOGY

SOURCES

Where our data comes from, what each source is used for, and how we keep the site independent.

01

CURRENT / SESSION DATA

OpenF1

Used as an optional enhancement for modern session-rich data. The public site now carries its own local current-season snapshot so pages do not fail when current OpenF1 access requires authentication.

Historical detailed session data is available from 2023 onward; real-time access may require a paid plan.
02

HISTORICAL DATABASE

F1DB

Primary historical layer for seasons, drivers, constructors, circuits, standings and race results from 1950 onward.

F1DB data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
03

EDITORIAL HISTORY

APEX archive

“On This Day” summaries and future editorial pages are written specifically for this site rather than copied from third-party articles.

StatsF1 and other established databases may be used manually to cross-check factual details.
04

REFERENCE ONLY

StatsF1

Useful as an independent verification reference and a benchmark for the depth of historical statistics.

We do not use StatsF1 as a scraped production API and do not copy its editorial text.
